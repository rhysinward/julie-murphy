Julie Murphy F.Inst.TT is Head of Trade Relations & Partnerships, EMEA at All Nippon Airways (ANA), responsible for the airline’s relationships with major travel partners across Europe. She has worked in airlines since 1987 and with ANA since 1997. She is an elected Board Director of the Institute of Travel & Tourism and elected Chair of Star Alliance UK & Ireland.
